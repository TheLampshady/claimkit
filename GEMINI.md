# claimkit

**Research that backs the work.** Every claim is either a cited finding or an explicit hypothesis carrying the check that would settle it; every action names which of those it rests on.

The skill auto-activates from its description — mention `claimkit`, or ask to research a topic, to force it.

> This file is read as workspace context by **Antigravity** (`GEMINI.md` or `AGENTS.md` at the workspace root).

## Output

Docs land at `docs/research/<topic>-research.md`. They are **living**: revise in place, never replace and never fork to a dated filename. Hypothesis and action IDs (`H1`, `A1`) are permanent once assigned.

## The charter

If `docs/research/CHARTER.md` exists, it's read for frame — scope, rails, evidence standard — and **never cited as a source**. Only its Terminology section may be appended automatically.
